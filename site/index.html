Your little studio for sound and video.

Trim, clean up, caption, mix and master on your own computer. Every change previews on a short clip first, so you hear before and after side by side before a two-hour file is touched.

What it does

Edit in one pass

Trim, cut the pauses, EQ, compress, fade and reframe for vertical. Stack the steps and render once.

Clean up audio

Remove noise, hum, harsh "s" sounds and clicks. Learn the noise from a quiet gap for a cleaner result.

Captions

Transcribe with Whisper, fix the text, translate it, then export subtitles or burn them in.

Mix and master

Music ducks under your voice by itself. Master with presets for podcasts and streaming, or match a reference track.

Downloads

Grab a video, an audio track, a playlist or just the captions from YouTube and many other sites, straight into your bins.

Your plugins

Load your VST3 effects, open their own windows, and use them like any other step.

Get started in three steps

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. No admin rights needed.

  2. Set up

    Pick where your media and models live. Setup fetches ffmpeg, Python and the tools you choose.

  3. Drop in a file

    Open it on the Desk, try a preset on a short clip, and render when it sounds right.

Everything stays on your machine.
